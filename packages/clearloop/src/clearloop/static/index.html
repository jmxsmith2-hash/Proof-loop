        Calibrate

        Measure on your own machine what ClearLoop won't guess. Each test turns a small plate you slice (any tiny part) into a test print that pauses between steps. Watch it, then press Resume on the printer.

        1 · Sweep floor

        After the part prints, remove it. The pusher then sweeps the empty plate at Z20, 15, 10, 8, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2.5, 2, 1.5 and 1 mm, pausing after each. Stop at the first height where it touches the plate: your floor is the height before it.

        Result

        mm

        2 · Cooldown

        Runs only the bed-temperature wait, then pauses. Read the bed temperature on the printer screen at the pause. If it's well above your target, the firmware stopped waiting early, so set a higher release temperature or add natural cooldown.

        °C target

        3 · Door (Stage 2)

        FarmBoard opens the door, the printer pauses, and after you press Resume the door closes. Confirms the board receives triggers from your printer.

        4 · Bend (Stage 2)

        Runs the FarmBoard bend with FarmLoop's own timing, then pauses. If the bender is still moving at the pause, it needs longer than the kit's built-in wait.

        Hardware

        A hardware profile holds the moves that belong to your FarmLoop kit: FarmBoard triggers, bend timing, the sweep path and the park position. ClearLoop never makes these up. Each profile shows where its values came from.

        Add your kit from a FarmLoop file

        Works for any printer and any FarmLoop stage, including Stage 1 from the free FarmLoop Lite plan. Slice a plate, process the same plate in the FarmLoop app, then give ClearLoop both files. It compares them, keeps your kit's moves exactly as FarmLoop wrote them, and leaves out FarmLoop's cooldown and sweep heights, which it does better.
