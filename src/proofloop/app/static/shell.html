    What's new

    LARP mode

    A second ProofLoop with pretend printers, to try anything on: slicing, loop files, sending, the queue, orders. It has a twin of each of your printers (same model, nozzle and AMS filament) and any pretend printers you add.

    It starts blank and keeps its own data: nothing you do in LARP touches your real printers, settings, queue, orders or history. Your real ProofLoop keeps watching your printers while this window shows LARP.

    Pretend printers

      A twin you remove isn't made again. Removing a printer here or under Printers ends what it was printing.

      Your printers on your phone

      Shows Loop files and Printers on your phone, over your own Wi-Fi: printer status, plate checks, filament and costs. Nothing goes through the internet, and only phones you pair can see it.

      Windows may ask whether Python can use the network: choose Private networks.

      On your phone, on the same Wi-Fi, scan the code for what that phone may do. Or open and type its six-digit code.

      Watch only

      Sees printers, plate checks, filament and costs. Can't change anything.

      Operator

      Can also change spools, orders, the queue and printers. Never sends a print.

      A paired phone stays paired. "Forget phones" unpairs all of them and makes new codes.
